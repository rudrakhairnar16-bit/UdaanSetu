**Product:** UdaanSetu

**Organization:** Government of Jharkhand

**Target:** SIH 2026  
**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Product Direction:** Problem → Research → Innovation → Industry → Deployment → Impact

---

# 1. Executive Summary

UdaanSetu will be transformed from an Innovation Ecosystem Management Platform into an **AI-powered Societal Problem-to-Impact Platform**.

The platform will allow citizens, communities and government stakeholders to submit societal challenges. AI will understand, classify, prioritize and deduplicate these challenges and then recommend suitable universities, departments, faculty, researchers, mentors, industry partners and funding opportunities.

Once a problem is accepted, UdaanSetu will manage the complete solution lifecycle:

**Problem → Validation → AI Analysis → University Matching → Research → Mentorship → Industry Collaboration → Funding → Prototype → Pilot → IPR → Startup/Deployment → Impact**

The existing UdaanSetu research, innovation, IPR, startup, mentor, funding, semantic matching, duplicate detection, analytics and RBAC capabilities will be retained and reoriented around this lifecycle.

# 2. Product Vision

## Vision

> **Turn real societal problems into collaborative, research-backed and measurable solutions.**

## Mission

Create a single digital ecosystem where:

- citizens can report meaningful problems;
- government can discover and prioritize challenges;
- universities can find real-world research opportunities;
- researchers and faculty can collaborate;
- industry can provide funding, technology and expertise;
- startups can participate in deployment;
- government can track outcomes and social impact.

---

# 3. Problem Statement

Societal problems are often:

- reported through disconnected channels;
- difficult to categorize;
- duplicated across locations;
- disconnected from appropriate universities;
- difficult to match with domain experts;
- disconnected from industry;
- difficult to fund;
- difficult to track after research begins;
- poorly measured in terms of real-world impact.

The platform therefore needs to establish a structured pipeline from **problem identification to measurable solution deployment**.

---

# 4. Product Goals

## Primary Goals

1. Crowdsource societal problems.
2. Automatically understand submitted problems using AI.
3. Categorize and prioritize problems.
4. Detect duplicate/similar problems.
5. Match problems to universities.
6. Match problems to faculty/researchers.
7. Build multidisciplinary research teams.
8. Match relevant industry/startup/CSR partners.
9. Track funding.
10. Manage research projects and milestones.
11. Track prototypes and pilots.
12. Manage IPR/patent lifecycle.
13. Track startup/deployment outcomes.
14. Measure social/economic/environmental impact.
15. Provide government decision-support dashboards.

---

# 5. Non-Goals

The MVP will NOT attempt to:

- become a general-purpose social network;
- replace university ERP systems;
- provide legally binding AI decisions;
- automatically approve government funding;
- guarantee AI-generated priority scores;
- replace human review;
- integrate every government API;
- build a production-scale national identity system.

AI recommendations must remain **decision-support**, not autonomous government decisions.

# 10. AI Priority Engine

Every problem receives a recommendation score.

### Inputs

- population affected;
- urgency;
- severity;
- geographic spread;
- recurrence;
- existing solution gap;
- government priority;
- feasibility;
- potential social impact.

### Output

```text

```

### Explainability

System must show:

```text
Why HIGH?

Population affected: High
Urgency: High
Recurrence: Medium
Solution gap: High
Potential impact: High
```
