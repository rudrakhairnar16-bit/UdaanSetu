## PART 1: Gujarat Government Portals & APIs

### 1. Gujarat Startup Policy & Implementation
- **Portal:** https://invest.gujarat.gov.in/
- **What:** Gujarat Industrial Policy 2020, startup registrations
- **Data available:**
  - Registered startups in Gujarat
  - Sector-wise distribution
  - District-wise count
  - Funding received
- **How to access:** RTI request or contact Gujarat Startup Cell
- **Contact:** startupcell-ind@investindia.gov.in

### 2. Gujarat Startup Registry
- **Portal:** https://startup.gujarat.gov.in/
- **What:** Official Gujarat startup registration data
- **Data available:**
  - Startup name, sector, district
  - Registration date, status
  - Founder details
- **How to access:** Apply via Right to Information (RTI) Act
- **RTI fee:** ₹10 online at https://rtionline.gujarat.gov.in/

### 3. Gujarat Industrial Policy 2020
- **URL:** https://invest.gujarat.gov.in/industrial-policy
- **Key incentives (for your pitch):**
  - 12% interest subsidy for 5 years
  - 100% stamp duty exemption
  - Patent filing reimbursement up to ₹10 lakhs
  - Seed funding up to ₹40 lakhs for startups

### 4. Gujarat Council of Science & Technology (GCOST)
- **URL:** https://gcost.gujarat.gov.in/
- **What:** Research project funding data
- **Data available:**
  - Funded research projects
  - Principal investigators
  - Institutions, sectors
  - Funding amounts
- **How to access:** RTI request

### 5. Gujarat Biotechnology University
- **URL:** https://gbu.ac.in/
- **What:** Biotech research projects in Gujarat
- **Data available:** Research papers, student projects

### 6. Gujarat Venture Finance Limited (GVFL)
- **URL:** https://www.gvfl.com/
- **What:** Gujarat's oldest VC fund
- **Data available:**
  - Portfolio companies
  - Funding rounds
  - Sector focus
- **How to access:** Annual reports on website

## PART 4: Gujarat Sector-Specific Data

### Agriculture (Gujarat's largest sector):
- **Source:** https://agriinfo.gov.in/gujarat
- **Data:** Crop production, farmer data, mandi prices
- **Source:** https://enam.gov.in/ (e-NAM for mandi data)
- **Data:** Daily prices, crop arrivals

### Textiles (Surat, Ahmedabad):
- **Source:** https://texprocil.org/ (Textile ministry)
- **Data:** Export data, production numbers

### Pharma (Ahmedabad — "Pharma Capital of India"):
- **Source:** https://oppaiindia.org/ (OPPI)
- **Data:** Pharma companies, drug approvals

### IT/ITeS (Gandhinagar GIFT City):
- **Source:** https://giftcity.com/
- **Data:** Companies registered, jobs created

### Dairy (Anand — "Milk Capital of India"):
- **Source:** https://amul.com/ (GCMMF)
- **Data:** Cooperative network, farmer data
